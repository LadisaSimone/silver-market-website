About Silver Market Intelligence

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Who runs this site

Silver Market Intelligence is an independent research summary published and maintained by Simone Ladisa. It is not a company, brokerage, or financial institution — it's a personal project built to make the daily silver market easier to read at a glance.

What this site does

Every day, the site pulls live silver price data, macro indicators (the US Dollar Index, the 10-year Treasury yield), technical signals (RSI, volatility), positioning data (CFTC Commitment of Traders), ETF holdings, and current news coverage. That data is then scored across several independent categories and combined into a single daily conviction score, an outlook summary, and a ranked list of the key drivers moving the market that day.

How the analysis works

The scoring is generated by an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic), prompted to analyze each category strictly on its own evidence — a category's score reflects only what that category's own data shows, not the overall market mood — and to cite a specific number for every judgment it makes, so nothing on the page is a vague or unsupported claim. The categories are then combined using a fixed weighting formula to produce the Overall score. The full pipeline, including which data sources feed it, is open about its limitations: when a data point isn't available on a given day, the site says so directly (see the "Data Availability" handling reflected in the daily briefing) rather than guessing.
